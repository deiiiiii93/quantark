Build Notes

  • Daily KI is modeled as 252 discrete business-day observations per year and scales with tenor.
  • The interest leg is valued as the selected protected, principal-excluded Snowball with KO rate fixed at 100% unannualized.
  • The HTML embeds a quadrature-solved cube using a 1001-point grid and interpolates between nodes in-browser.
  • This is a pricing explainer, not a production quoting front-end.
