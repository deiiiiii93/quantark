Existing test expectations changed (3, all in test/test_accumulator_mc_engine.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reason (all three): a numeric accumulator at maturity 1e-12 (is_zero) still holds its last observation at t = 1e-12,
i.e. AT the valuation instant. The engines' expiry shortcut dropped it; it is now decided on the spot exactly as
``settle_valuation_instant`` decides an observation at the valuation instant on every other day (theta boundary R1,
ruling D2: a fixing at t = 0 is live). Without this the dated accumulator on its exercise date (now priced) would
silently pay nothing for its last day.
