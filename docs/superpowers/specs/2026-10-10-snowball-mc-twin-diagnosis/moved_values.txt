W17a moved values vs c02296cb (branch w17-snowball-mc-twin)
==========================================================

Every value below moved because its c02296cb MC time grid held a step below Tolerance.ZERO (1e-10 y): two spellings
of one instant kept as two nodes. The merge makes them one node; the move is a fresh draw of the same law (the
empty step re-dealt every random draw after it), not a change of the priced contract. Every grid without such a
step is bit-identical (test_mc_time_node_merge.py::test_grids_without_a_collision_are_bit_identical_to_the_set_
construction, every valuation day, both forms; and 1828 of the 1940 measured rows are unchanged to the bit).

(w17a_after_branch.txt); summarize_w17a.py -> w17a_moved_rows.txt (all 112 moved rows, old -> new, with the
