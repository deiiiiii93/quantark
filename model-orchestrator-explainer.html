Design Philosophy
设计理念

6 Core Principles6 大核心原则

Architectural decisions behind the orchestrator, drawn from SR 11-7 model risk management best practices. 协调器背后的架构决策，源自 SR 11-7 模型风险管理最佳实践。

01
Independence独立性
Developer B never reads Developer A's code. True independent re-implementation means correctness is verified, not just copied.开发者 B 绝不阅读开发者 A 的代码。真正独立的重新实现确保正确性经过验证，而非简单复制。
02
Traceability可追溯性
Every decision is logged in tasks.md. Gate failures, rollbacks, and review outcomes create a complete audit trail for regulators.每个决策都记录在 tasks.md 中。门禁失败、回滚和评审结果为监管机构提供完整审计跟踪。
03
Gates Matter门禁至关重要
A failed gate forces rollback — not a warning to be ignored. After 3 failures, the pipeline escalates to the human for a decision.门禁失败强制回滚，而非被忽略的警告。3 次失败后，流水线升级至人工决策。
04
Parallel Reviews并行评审
Performance, security, and code quality reviews run concurrently after the gate. Three reviewers, same clock — no sequential bottleneck.性能、安全和代码质量评审在门禁通过后并发执行。三个评审者，相同时钟——无顺序瓶颈。
05
Human in Control人工掌控
Key decisions require human approval: rollback vs abort, review issues, MC skip, final sign-off. AI executes — humans decide.关键决策需要人工批准：回滚还是中止、评审问题、MC 跳过、最终签署。AI 执行，人类决策。
06
Complete Package完整验证包
The output is VALIDATION-PACKAGE.md — not just code. Executive summary, all sub-reports, and a formal production approval decision.输出是 VALIDATION-PACKAGE.md，不仅是代码，还包含执行摘要、所有子报告和正式生产批准决定。
