Does a converging tail beat hold-forward?

1 of 3 converging tails beat hold-forward in both books. METHOD

Setup, marks, costs and caveats

78 run files read.

Trade
Short 2Y standard snowball, notional 10,000,000 CNY, strike = KO ref = inception spot, KO 100% monthly from month 3 to 24, KI 75% discrete daily, coupon = KO coupon = rebate, principal excluded.
Inceptions
Quarterly, one coupon per inception solved to PV = 0 under hold-forward and shared by all tails.
Booking
Each tail books at its own day-0 PV; the day-0 model gap is excluded from P&L (see #hedging).
Tails
hold forward, hold carry yield, zero carry, converge to a trailing 63-day far-IM anchor with half-lives 0.25, 0.5, 1Y (252-day anchor as a sensitivity).
Marks
Spot = CSI 1000 close; IM = CFFEX close; MO = CFFEX settlement; flat r = 1.4%, ACT/365F.
Hedges
Book A: far IM only (follows the longest IM). Book B: IM pillar lots plus MO C−P pair at its opened strike and the pinned-forward delta in the far IM. Multipliers IM 200, MO pair 100·DF(T_n).
Costs
IM 1 bp of traded notional; MO 0.2 pt per option leg per side (40 CNY per pair). Variance is gross of costs.
Engine
QUAD V2 (defaults), flat vol per day from the chain; V1 parity check on inception day.
Std
Pooled std = sqrt(mean per-inception daily variance), equal weight per inception.
Terminal
At the traded price: 0 − realised note cash + realised hedge P&L + cash accrual; own-PV version alongside.

Caveats

  • Overlapping inceptions. 2Y notes struck a quarter apart share most of their calendar, so daily hedged P&L is strongly correlated across inceptions and the paired tests overstate the independent evidence.
  • Truncation. Notes not yet knocked out or matured at the end of the data are truncated; daily statistics use their live days, the terminal measure uses completed runs only (under 5 completed is not decidable).
  • Early knock-outs. Hold-forward run endings: data_end 2, ko 11. A knock-out ends the hedge early, so those daily samples are short and the terminal sample is small.
  • Unhedged vega. Neither book hedges vega; its daily P&L is common to all tails, which is why a vega-adjusted residual is shown as a diagnostic. Share of raw hedged variance removed by the vega adjustment (mean over tails): Book A 27%, Book B 19%.
  • One flat vol. No smile, local or stochastic vol; the vol used each day is the one the chain implies.
  • Basis is not modelled. The curve is built from marks; the realised basis is in the P&L, which is the point.
