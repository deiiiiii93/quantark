What was tested and how

The desk sold a 2-year snowball on the CSI 1000 index (000852.SH) at price 0 and hedges it. Everything on this page is the seller's: P&L, position delta and gamma, and cash are the seller's, in bp of notional (10 mio CNY per note) unless stated. The primary hedge is the delta in one IM future (the longest listed contract with at least 7 days left, the central 1% bump delta of the true note); the secondary layer is MO options near a barrier. Prices and greeks come from the QUAD V2 engine with one flat volatility per day (the day's far-MO at-the-money vol).

  • The note. Strike = KO reference = booking-day spot; knock-out at 100% of strike on the monthly anniversaries from month 3 to month 24, paying the coupon; knock-in at 75% of strike observed on every daily close; principal excluded. The coupon is solved so the note is worth 0 under hold-forward carry.
  • Two books. ki75 is the 2Y product at real inception dates (the quarterly headline set plus notes struck so that real KO observations land near the barrier); KO pin events are measured on it. seas6 is the same 75% product in its last 6 months (18 months elapsed, coupon accrued over the full 24, not knocked in), booked on the first trading day of each ISO week with its KI barrier 7% below that day's spot; KI approaches are measured on it, because the knock-in pin is a property of a short remaining life (section 04).
  • Events. A KO pin event is a KO observation with the prior close within 2% of the barrier. A KI approach is a run of trading days with spot within 5% above the KI barrier that comes within 3% or ends in a knock-in. Train / test split by event date at 2025-07-01: parameters were picked on training events, the verdict is read on test events only.
  • Windows. KO: the daily rows from the first trading day on which the observation is at most 10 trading days ahead through the trading day after it. KI: from the approach's first day through the trading day after its last day. Each window carries its entry and exit trading costs.
  • Costs and limits. IM costs 1 bp of traded notional; MO costs 0.2 index points per leg per side. The IM hedge ratio (IM notional / note notional) is clipped to [−0.25, 3.0]. MO legs are opened only with day volume ≥ 100 and open interest ≥ 500 lots and are checked at a desk of 500 mio CNY: a trade is cut to 10% of the day's volume and a series position to 1,200 lots per side.

Three execution clocks

  • close (decides the verdict). every trade fills at the day's close (IM close, MO settlement): the 14:55 day-end order proxy. It is the pre-registered primary clock; it lets the seller trade at the fixing price itself, so it understates the exposure of a true pin.
  • twap (a VWAP proxy). every trade fills at the day VWAP (turnover / volume / multiplier), close at zero volume; a VWAP proxy for a TWAP order; trade size comes from the close, so it is correlated with close minus VWAP. Option legs fill at settlement under this clock too.
  • next_open (stress). as close, except on fixing days: no IM trade at the fixing close, the adjustment fills at the next IM open and option legs at the next settlement. It overstates the exposure of a pin; a fixing on the last data day has no next open and its event is left out of the next_open tables (counted).

The pre-registered verdict rule

  • (a) Tail, on test events under close: ES10 of the event-window P&L higher than the baseline's, worst event not lower, and week-clustered bootstrap p < 0.10 (10,000 replicates, seed 20261112).
  • (b) Whole life, on the unselected notes of the same book (KO: the ki75 headline set; KI: every seas6 booking), day 0 left out: daily std at most 1.05 x the baseline's and mean terminal P&L at least the baseline's minus 10 bp.
  • A technique wins with (a) and (b); decidable only with at least 20 test events in at least 10 distinct weeks; robust = a winner that also passes (a) under twap and next_open. Raw p decides; Holm-adjusted p is printed beside it.

Days the hedge could not trade at the close

On a day the far IM contract closed at its daily price limit, a sale (at a limit down) or a purchase (at a limit up) at the close was not executable. The close clock books it anyway; the twap clock fills the close-state size at the day's VWAP, which no desk could have known at the time. The twap improvement of the KI tail on such days is therefore not an achievable fill. On 2024-09-30 (up, locked) the VWAP was 2.3% below the close and the next open was 10.0% from the limit close. On 2025-04-07 (down, locked) the VWAP was 2.3% above the close and the next open was 0.3% from the limit close. Where the next open was within 1% of the limit close the close figure is about right, by luck: 2025-04-07.

Knock-in or rebound: a directional part of the KI P&L

Rules that carry less long futures into the KI barrier (ki_wrong, the raised-barrier control; ki_cap; secant at a wide bump) gain when the approach ends in a knock-in and pay when the index rebounds, so their mean P&L is partly a directional outcome of how many approaches knocked in (43 knock-ins and 36 rebounds here). The whole-life test catches this only through the std criterion, because the terminal criterion is one-sided (it only asks that a technique not lose more than the slack).

Why the KI book is seasoned

The knock-in pin is the jump in the note's value and in its hedge when the KI flag flips. For a fresh note 3% above its KI barrier it is large only with a short life left: with a long life left a note near its barrier knocks in later anyway, so the flag is worth little, and a higher barrier makes that more certain. That is why the KI verdict is read on a 75% note in its last 6 months, not on a note with a lifted barrier.

GAMMA

LIMITS

Limits, stated plainly

  • Limit days. On a day the far IM contract closed at its daily price limit the close clock books a trade that could not be executed and the twap clock fills it at a VWAP no desk could have known: the twap improvement of the KI tail on such days is not an achievable fill (section 03). Limit closes in the data: 2024-02-06 (up), 2024-09-27 (up), 2024-09-30 (up, locked), 2025-04-07 (down, locked). The KI tail is concentrated in single market days: 4 of the 8 tail approaches (Test slice) are dated 2026-03-23 (section 03).
  • The futures position limit binds every futures target (hedge ratio in [−0.25, 3.0]), including the hedge of option legs left on the book after a note has ended: such a hedge is clipped and counted like any other. Rows after the note's end (the residual legs hedged), Test slice, close clock: mo_ko@2,payoff 204 rows on 92 notes; sop_overlay 204 rows on 92 notes. The run files store the clip count per whole life, not per row, so how many of these rows were clipped is not shown.
  • The carried fill ratio is reset when an overlay closes. A day whose target wants no leg and carries no refusal sets the carried fill ratio to 0, so the residual of a closed overlay covers nothing on a later refusal day; that is why the minimum fill ratio of an overlay technique can read 0%.
  • Scenario sizing mostly hits the 3x cap. Unless the option series ends within about a week of the observation, the scenario lot count is capped at 3 times the payoff lot count, so mo_ko@1,scenario is in practice about 3 x the payoff lots.
  • Overlay strikes. An overlay is refused when the barrier is more than one listed strike step beyond the listing; within one step it is built, and its payout kink then sits up to one listed strike step from the barrier.
  • Carried coverage. On a day an overlay is refused while legs are held, the coverage ratio of the last sized day is carried; it is not rescaled for expiries or partial unwinds since that day.
  • freeze inside the overlay composite holds the hedge carried, legs included, which is exact only while the legs do not change that day.
  • The seasoned bookings are synthetic bookings on real paths: real spot, vol, IM and MO marks, but bookings the desk did not make on those dates; weekly bookings overlap in calendar time, so their events are not independent. The 2Y product's own KI approaches are two episodes with a small gap.
  • Clocks. close lets the seller trade at the fixing price itself and understates a true pin; next_open overstates it; twap uses close-state targets with average-price fills, and its trade size is correlated with close minus VWAP. The option VWAP is not used as a fill (it is outside the day's range on thin contracts).
  • The seasoned maturity payment is about 2 years of coupon (coupon x (1.5 + T) with T the rolled maturity in years, up to about 1% away from 2.0), not exactly 2.0.
  • Intraday rules (an exit at the closing auction, a pin band on the fixing day) are carried from earlier studies and not re-tested here.
  • The MO series limit of 1,200 lots per side is the listing-time CFFEX client limit; its current value was not verified. One flat vol per day: no smile, no vega hedge.
