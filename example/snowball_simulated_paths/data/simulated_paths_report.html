Day-0 book marks

Every cell books the traded price (0) at inception, and the coupon is fair under the reference carry model on QUAD. A cell's day-0 mark is its own carry model's price of that contract on its provider, so it holds the carry-model gap (zero only under the reference model) plus the engine gap; the engine part is the difference against the cell's exact-QUAD check, which starts from the same state. The mark is carried in every terminal P&L of the cell.

Caveats

  • Every simulated path starts from one state, the history's last day, so a historical inception's percentile is indicative: its own start state differs.
  • Under the life surface and the spot ladder the term dividend models enter only through the scalar yield at the remaining maturity, flat at the bucket centre; under per_date and exact every state hands the engine that date's term dividend object.
  • Paired t-statistics treat the simulated paths as independent draws, unlike the historical study's overlapping inceptions.
  • The stress paths are designed, not sampled; they show mechanics, not probabilities.
