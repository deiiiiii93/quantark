Can China’s onshore FX market support Heston calibration?

§1 · Decision

Calibration-ready is not calibration-proven

The artifact set contains 30 observed five-delta nodes across 6 selected tenors. That is enough cross-sectional geometry to fit Heston, but the source remains a public composite. The defensible conclusion is therefore conditional: USD/CNY supports the calibration exercise; promotion requires executable-history and stability evidence.

§5 · Dynamics

Local volatility and SLV answer a different question

Local volatility asks for the diffusion that reproduces the prepared vanilla surface. SLV adds stochastic variance and a leverage function. Their calibration diagnostics test the prepared surface and numerical engines; they do not create additional observed market information.

Local-vol raw in-domain RMSE0.2132 vol ptsfrom cfets_usdcny_localvol
SLV raw in-domain RMSE0.3122 vol ptsfrom cfets_usdcny_slv
Heston observed-node RMSE0.0592 vol ptssame observed five-delta language
InterpretationThe Heston gate is scored directly on observed five-delta nodes. Local-vol and SLV are downstream diagnostics against a smoothed surface; their errors are not independent evidence of market depth, and the public sample does not justify promoting SLV to production calibration.
Vanilla fit is not an exotic validationA snapshot fit does not test the daily fixing, managed-band regime, CNY/CNH basis dynamics, or intervention jumps that can matter for barriers and fixing-sensitive payoffs.

  1. Public bid/mid/ask are CFETS composite outputs, not node-level executable quotes.
  2. A displayed zero spread must not be interpreted as an executable zero-spread market.
  3. Official turnover evidence supports the <=1Y core much more strongly than 18M-3Y.
  4. The rectangular grid is model-based interpolation; the optional union domain also requires per-tenor extrapolation.
  5. Adjacent maturity slices are included only as finite-difference boundary support and are excluded from calibration scoring.
  6. Local-vol and SLV results are therefore smoothing-sensitive diagnostics, not direct market observations.
  7. Expiry and delivery are treated as equal in the native FX local-vol v1 diagnostic.
  8. PDE error and SABR/calendar preparation error are both present in raw-node RMSE.
  9. SLV inherits the stage-02 smile interpolation and boundary-support assumptions.
  10. Monte Carlo standard errors are sampling diagnostics, not executable quote spreads.
  11. The uniform-grid SLV PDE probe diagnoses engine resolution and is not market evidence.
